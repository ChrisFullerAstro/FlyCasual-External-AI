# X-Wing Tactics Guide

## Fundamental Concepts

### Arc Dodging
- Position your ship outside enemy firing arcs while keeping them in yours
- High initiative ships arc dodge better (move after enemies)
- Use boost and barrel roll to fine-tune final position

### Focus Fire
- Concentrate attacks on one enemy ship to destroy it quickly
- A destroyed ship deals no damage; a damaged ship deals full damage
- Half-killing multiple ships is worse than fully killing one

### Action Economy
- More actions = more advantage
- Coordinate gives an ally a free action
- Linked actions give a second action (often stressed)
- Bumping denies actions - use it offensively

### Blocking
- Land your ship where an enemy wants to go
- The enemy bumps you and loses their action
- Low initiative ships are best blockers (move first)
- Predict enemy maneuvers based on their position and options

## Range Control

### Range 1 Engagement
- Attacker gets +1 die, best for high-attack ships
- Dangerous if multiple enemies can shoot back
- Ideal for finishing damaged ships

### Range 3 Engagement
- Defender gets +1 die, best for high-agility ships
- Safer for fragile ships
- Use when outnumbered to reduce incoming damage

## Ship Roles

### Ace
- High initiative, high agility, expensive
- Arc dodge and pick fights carefully
- Use focus/evade for defense
- Examples: Vader, Fenn Rau, Wedge

### Jouster
- Efficient attack/health ratio, lower initiative
- Fly straight at enemies, trade damage
- Use focus for offense
- Examples: X-Wings, TIE Fighters

### Blocker
- Low initiative, expendable
- Fly into enemy paths to deny actions
- Doesn't need to survive, just disrupt
- Examples: Academy TIE, Bandit Squadron Z-95

### Support
- Provides buffs to allies
- Stay safe while coordinating or buffing
- Examples: Ships with coordinate, squadron leader abilities

## Common Mistakes

### Spreading Fire
- Attacking different enemies each turn
- Leaves multiple damaged ships that all shoot back
- Focus fire instead

### Predictable Movement
- Always doing the same maneuver
- Easy to block or arc dodge
- Mix up maneuvers, use unexpected moves

### Ignoring Stress
- Taking red maneuvers without purpose
- Stressed ships can't act or K-turn
- Plan blue maneuvers to clear stress

### Chasing Arc Dodgers
- Following a high-initiative ace around
- They move after you and dodge your arc
- Instead: block their path, use turrets, or ignore and kill their friends

## Engagement Tips

### The Approach
- Don't joust an enemy with better joust (more efficient firepower)
- Flank to get shots without return fire
- Use obstacles to block enemy approach lanes

### The Furball
- Once engaged, focus fire to remove ships quickly
- K-turn behind enemies who flew past you
- Don't flee unless very damaged - turning back loses tempo

### The Endgame
- With advantage: trade efficiently, don't take risks
- Behind on points: take risks to kill valuable targets
- Watch the clock in tournaments
