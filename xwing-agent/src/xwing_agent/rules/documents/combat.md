# Combat Rules

## Attack Sequence
1. **Declare Target**: Choose a ship in your firing arc and range
2. **Roll Attack Dice**: Roll dice equal to your attack value
3. **Defender Rolls Defense Dice**: Roll dice equal to agility value
4. **Modify Dice**: Both players may modify dice (attacker first)
5. **Neutralize Results**: Evades cancel hits, then crits
6. **Deal Damage**: Remaining hits deal facedown damage, crits deal faceup damage

## Range
- **Range 0**: Ships overlapping/touching - CAN attack with primary weapon but NO dice modifications allowed (X-Wing 2.5 rule)
- **Range 1**: Close range - attacker rolls +1 attack die (no bonus at Range 0)
- **Range 2**: Medium range - no modifier
- **Range 3**: Long range - defender rolls +1 defense die
- **Range 4+**: Beyond range - cannot attack with primary weapons

## Firing Arcs
- **Front Arc**: 80° forward arc (±40° from centerline)
- **Rear Arc**: 80° arc behind the ship (±40° from rear centerline)
- **Full Front Arc**: 180° forward hemisphere
- **Bullseye Arc**: Narrow arc directly ahead (used for special abilities)
- **Turret Arc**: Mobile arc that can be rotated

## Attack Dice Results
- **Hit**: Deals 1 damage (cancelled by evade)
- **Critical Hit**: Deals 1 faceup damage (cancelled by evade after regular hits)
- **Focus**: No effect unless modified
- **Blank**: No effect

## Defense Dice Results
- **Evade**: Cancels one hit or crit
- **Focus**: No effect unless modified
- **Blank**: No effect

## Damage
- Damage is dealt to shields first
- When shields are depleted, damage goes to hull
- Regular hits deal facedown damage cards
- Critical hits deal faceup damage cards (with negative effects)
- Ship is destroyed when hull damage equals or exceeds hull value

## Primary vs Special Weapons
- Primary weapon: Standard ship attack
- Special weapons: Missiles, torpedoes, cannons with specific requirements
- Special weapons often require spending a lock and/or charges
