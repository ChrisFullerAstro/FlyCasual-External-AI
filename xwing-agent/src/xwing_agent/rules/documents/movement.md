# Movement Rules

## Maneuver Dial
Each ship has a unique maneuver dial showing available maneuvers. During Planning Phase, secretly set each ship's dial.

## Maneuver Types
- **Straight**: Move directly forward
- **Bank**: Gentle 45° turn while moving forward
- **Turn**: Sharp 90° turn
- **Koiogran Turn (K-Turn)**: Move forward then rotate 180° (ends facing backward)
- **Segnor's Loop (S-Loop)**: Bank maneuver that rotates 180°
- **Tallon Roll**: Turn maneuver that rotates 180°
- **Reverse**: Move backward (rare)
- **Stationary**: Stay in place (speed 0)

## Maneuver Speeds
- Speed 0: Stationary only
- Speed 1-5: Various maneuvers available depending on ship

## Maneuver Difficulty
- **Blue**: Easy maneuver - removes 1 stress token
- **White**: Normal maneuver - no effect
- **Red**: Difficult maneuver - gains 1 stress token

## Stress and Red Maneuvers
- A stressed ship cannot execute red maneuvers
- If a stressed ship's dial shows a red maneuver, it executes a white 2-straight instead
- A stressed ship cannot perform actions (except certain special actions)

## Overlapping Ships (Bumping)
- If a ship's final position overlaps another ship, it's placed touching but not overlapping
- A ship that bumps cannot perform its action
- Ships can attack ships they're touching at Range 0

## Overlapping Obstacles
- **Asteroids**: Skip action, roll attack die - on hit take 1 damage
- **Debris**: Gain 1 stress, roll attack die - on crit take 1 damage
- **Gas Clouds**: Roll 3 attack dice - suffer damage/strain for each focus result

## Flying Off the Board (Fleeing)
- A ship that moves off the play area is destroyed
- The ship is removed and the opponent scores its points
